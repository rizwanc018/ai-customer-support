# Paddle boards

## Sizes
We sell three inflatable paddle boards. Pick the size by rider weight, including gear.

- 10'6" All-round: riders up to 85 kg. For beginners and calm water.
- 11' All-round: riders up to 110 kg. Our most stable board, also good for two light riders.
- 12'6" Touring: riders up to 130 kg. Faster and longer, for trips and experienced paddlers.

If a rider is between two sizes, take the larger board.

## What is in the box
Every board comes with:

- Board
- 3-piece adjustable paddle (PB-PADDLE)
- Double-action hand pump with gauge (PB-PUMP)
- Removable center fin (PB-FIN)
- Coiled leash (PB-LEASH)
- Valve wrench (PB-WRENCH)
- Carry bag (PB-BAG)

## Inflation
Inflate to 12 to 15 PSI. Below 12 PSI the board bends in the middle.

Losing 1 to 2 PSI overnight is normal as the air cools. It is not a leak.

## Valve check for slow leaks
1. Inflate the board to 15 PSI.
2. Spray soapy water on the valve. Bubbles mean air escapes there.
3. Turn the valve core a quarter turn clockwise with the valve wrench.
4. Spray again, then check the pressure after 24 hours.

If the board still loses more than 2 PSI in 24 hours, ask for the order number, board size, and the number of days since delivery.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one.

- Missing accessory: we ship the part. No return needed.
- Slow leak: do the valve check first.
- Still leaking after the valve check, within 30 days of delivery: we send a new board.
- Still leaking after the valve check, more than 30 days after delivery: we send a patch kit.
- Split seam: always goes to a colleague.

To arrange anything, collect the order number, board size, and a short description of the problem.
