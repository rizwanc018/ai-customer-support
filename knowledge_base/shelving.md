# Shelving

## Models and finishes
We sell four flat-pack models. Each comes in natural oak, white, black, and walnut.

- HP-S3: 3-shelf bookcase, 106 cm high.
- HP-S5: 5-shelf bookcase, 176 cm high.
- HP-C4: 2x2 cube unit, 77 cm high.
- HP-C9: 3x3 cube unit, 112 cm high.

The model code is printed on the assembly manual and on the box label.

## Parts lists
Every panel has a number sticker. The part number is the model code plus the panel number, for example HP-S3-04.

- HP-S3 (6 panels): 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4 shelf, 5 bottom, 6 back panel.
- HP-S5 (9 panels): 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4 to 6 shelves, 7 bottom, 8 back panel, 9 kick plate.
- HP-C4 (7 panels): 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5 center divider, 6 shelf, 7 back panel.
- HP-C9 (9 panels): 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5 and 6 dividers, 7 and 8 shelves, 9 back panel.

Hardware kits: HK-S for the S models, HK-C for the C models. Each kit holds all cam locks, dowels, screws, and the wall anchor.

## Assembly tips
1. Lay the panels on the box or a rug so they do not scratch.
2. Point the cam lock arrow at the cam bolt hole. Turn clockwise until it stops, about half a turn.
3. Fit the back panel with the finished side facing into the shelf.
4. Fix tall units (HP-S5, HP-C9) to the wall with the included anchor.

Assembly takes 30 to 60 minutes for one person. A cordless screwdriver on a low setting is fine.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one.

- Missing screws or hardware: we ship the hardware kit for the model.
- 1 or 2 damaged panels: we ship those panels by part number.
- 3 or more damaged panels, or a damaged frame: we send a full replacement.
- Wrong finish delivered: we exchange it for the right finish.

To arrange anything, collect the order number, the model, and the part numbers of damaged panels. A photo helps.
