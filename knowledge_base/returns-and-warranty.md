# Returns and warranty

## Returns
All orders go through Amazon, so returns do too. The customer starts the return in Your Orders on Amazon within 30 days of delivery. Amazon sends the label and handles the refund.

We cannot start, speed up, or approve a return or refund. A colleague answers every refund question.

## Warranty
- Paddle boards: 2 years on the board, seams, and valve. 1 year on the paddle, pump, fin, and leash.
- Shelving: 2 years on panels and hardware.

The warranty covers manufacturing faults only, not damage from use, such as a puncture from a rock.

## Other products
The same rules apply to any other Harbor & Pine product: returns through Amazon within 30 days of delivery, and a 2-year warranty on manufacturing faults.
